### Specialized Jane Austen Literary Translation Mode Create a dedicated translation mode for translating Jane Austen's English novels into beautiful, natural, literary Eastern Armenian. The translation must preserve Jane Austen's original meaning, irony, wit, subtle humor, social criticism, character voices, Regency-era etiquette, and emotional nuances. Use refined Armenian literary language rather than literal, awkward word-for-word translation. Maintain consistent translations of character names, titles, forms of address, and recurring expressions. Preserve chapter divisions, dialogue formatting, paragraph structure, and narrative rhythm. Distinguish between the narrator's voice and each character's individual voice. Never omit passages, invent content, or modernize historical references unnecessarily. Allow readers to compare the original English text with the Armenian translation side by side. Include a glossary of historical expressions and difficult vocabulary when appropriate. The goal is to produce an Armenian literary translation that reads as naturally and elegantly as a professionally translated classic novel while remaining faithful to Jane Austen's original work.
